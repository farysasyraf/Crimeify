crime-by-state.csv: crime by state and type, for years data.gov.my has no police district figures for yet

Each state's (the police contingent's) crimes of each type in 2022, 2023 and 2024: murder, rape, robbery and causing
injury (violent crime), and break-in, lorry and van theft, motorcar theft, motorcycle theft and other theft (property
crime). "php artisan map:import-state-crime", and "php artisan map:import-crime" after it loads data.gov.my's file,
add them to dbo.CrimeStats for the years data.gov.my's police district figures don't cover, 2024 so far, with
Malaysia's added up from them. Years data.gov.my has come from data.gov.my, by police district.

Source:    Royal Malaysia Police (Polis Diraja Malaysia), "Crime Index" tables A to D: crime index by contingent,
           by contingent and category, and violent and property crime by contingent and type of crime, Malaysia,
           2022-2024. From the file "Crime Index.xlsx".

As the tables have them:
  - Sabah includes W.P. Labuan, and W.P. Kuala Lumpur includes W.P. Putrajaya, as in data.gov.my's figures.
  - Robbery is one figure, where data.gov.my has four kinds (by a gang or not, armed or not).
  - "House break-in & theft" is break-in, and "Other thefts" other theft.
  - A dash is none.

Checked when this file was made:
  - Every state's types add up to its violent and property crime in table B, and those to its crime index in table A.
  - The 2022 and 2023 figures are the same as data.gov.my's for every state and type (robbery's four kinds added up).
  - In table D's 2024 rows, the three vehicle theft columns hold motorcar, motorcycle and lorry theft, in that order,
    rather than the headings' lorry, motorcar and motorcycle: read as headed, every state's lorry theft would rise
    several times over and its motorcycle theft fall by nine tenths from 2023. This file has them under their types,
    for example Johor's lorry theft 108, motorcar 526 and motorcycle 1,380 (2023: 69, 415 and 1,290).
  - The tables' own total cells for 2024 are formulas pointing at the wrong rows, so the totals were added up instead.
