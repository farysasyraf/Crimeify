police-stations.csv: the police stations the Map page lists under the map, to start from

One row per police district in police-districts.csv: the police station its pin is on, with the region (ISO 3166-2
code) and police district it's listed under, its name, and its address and phone number where OpenStreetMap has them.
Where the pin is only on a town centre (26 districts), the station is the district's headquarters under its usual
name, "Ibu Pejabat Polis Daerah …", without an address or phone number. Nothing is filled in that the source doesn't
give: 22 stations have an address and 18 a phone number, as OpenStreetMap had them in September 2026, written as it
writes them. Check them before relying on them.

"php artisan map:import-stations" adds these to dbo.PoliceStations for each police district that has no station
there yet. After that, administrators keep them up to date on the Police stations page (Dev Module), which never
writes to this file.

Source:    OpenStreetMap (osm_place gives each station's OpenStreetMap object), looked up in September 2026 through
           the Overpass API
