police-districts.csv: where each police district's pin goes on the Map page

One row per police district in the crime data from data.gov.my, under its current name (see 'renamed' in
Modules/Map/Config/config.php), with the region it's shown in: Labuan's district counts under Sabah and
Putrajaya's under Kuala Lumpur in the crime data, but their pins are in Labuan (MY-15) and Putrajaya (MY-16).

located_at says what the point is:
  district police HQ  the district's police headquarters (Ibu Pejabat Polis Daerah), 113 districts
  police station      a police station in the district's main town, where the HQ isn't on the map, 16 districts
  town centre         the centre of the district's main town, where neither is on the map, 26 districts

Source:    OpenStreetMap (osm_place gives each point's OpenStreetMap object), looked up in September 2026
           through the Overpass API and Nominatim. Every point was checked to fall inside its region on the
           Map page's boundary map.
