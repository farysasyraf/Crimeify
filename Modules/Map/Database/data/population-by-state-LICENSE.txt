population-by-state.csv: each state's and federal territory's population, to turn crime into crime per 100,000 people

Each region's whole population (both sexes, all ages, all ethnic groups) in each year from 2016, in thousands, in
DOSM's own columns. "php artisan map:import-population" loads it into dbo.Populations; with --download, it loads
DOSM's latest file instead, which it reads the same way.

Source:    Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM), "Population Table: States" (population_state), OpenDOSM,
           https://open.dosm.gov.my/data-catalogue/population_state, from
           https://storage.dosm.gov.my/population/population_state.csv (last changed 31 July 2026), downloaded on
           9 October 2026.

Made by keeping the file's rows with sex "both", age "overall" and ethnicity "overall", from 2016 on, unchanged.

As DOSM has them:
  - Each year is the population at the middle of the year.
  - 2020 is the 2020 census, adjusted; the other years are estimates, each from the year before (births, deaths
    and migration). 2016 to 2019 were estimated from the 2010 census and weren't revised after the 2020 one, so some
    regions step in 2020: Sabah's falls from 3.90 to 3.42 million and Sarawak's from 2.81 to 2.45 million, and
    Selangor's rises from 6.51 to 6.99 million. Their crime per 100,000 people steps the other way that year.
  - W.P. Labuan and W.P. Putrajaya are counted on their own, not in Sabah and W.P. Kuala Lumpur.
