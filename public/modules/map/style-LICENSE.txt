style.json: the street map under the Map page (map.js), for MapLibre GL JS

Crimeify's own colours, dark and blue like the app, on the layers of OpenFreeMap's "dark" style, with place names in
Latin script only and without the town dots its icons don't have. Made from https://tiles.openfreemap.org/styles/dark
on 2026-11-11, recoloured layer by layer. To bring in a newer version, recolour its layers the same way.

Tiles:     OpenFreeMap, https://openfreemap.org: free vector tiles of OpenStreetMap, without a key or a limit.
           The map credits "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles Data from OpenStreetMap", as the tiles give it.
Data:      © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Style:     OpenFreeMap's styles are made from OpenMapTiles' styles: their code is BSD 3-Clause and their design
           CC-BY 4.0, https://github.com/openmaptiles. Fonts (Noto Sans) and the remaining icons come from
           OpenFreeMap's servers, as the style names them.
